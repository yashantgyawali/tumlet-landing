Board games in Nepal, especially in Kathmandu, are no longer just for kids. 

These days you can spot people playing games all over the city. Walk into a chiya pasal and you might see a group deep into an online game of Ludo.

Over the past few years, game nights have popped up in cafés, bars, and offices. People now meet to sit around a table, laugh, and PLAY, not only to eat.

This guide covers game nights, cafés with games, and local Nepali board games you can buy.

## 1. Board Game Nights in Nepal

### Tumlet Game Nights

Tumlet is a Nepali board game brand that makes games for young adults with high replay value. We want to grow the board game culture in Nepal, so we host free game nights every month.

We bring a mix of Nepali games and bestsellers, and we love teaching them to new players. You can meet people and try games you've never seen before.

Or just play for the fun of it.

📩 **Note:** We usually host game nights on the first Friday of each month. [DM us on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/tumlet.boardgames/) to find out when the next one is.

### Nextdoor: Free Game Night Every Thursday

Nextdoor, in Sanepa, runs a free board game night every Thursday from 6 PM. You can just walk in, pick a game, and join a table. If you're new, they'll happily teach you how to play.

It's a friendly space open to anyone, whatever your experience. It's a good place to wind down, meet new people, and learn some popular games.

📱 [Follow them on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/stay.nextdoor/?hl=en) for updates.

## 2. Cafés and Restaurants with Board Games

Several cafés in Kathmandu keep board games ready for customers. You only pay for your food or drinks, and the games are free to play.

### The Chiya Spot

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/thechiyaspot/?hl=en)

With several branches around the city, The Chiya Spot is a hangout for tea lovers. You'll often see a group balancing a Jenga tower while sipping tea. I love their seating options. You can even sit on the floor, which is perfect for board games.

### Mellow Bakeshop

📍 [Google Maps](https://share.google/Q6ZlR8tW7Ag2OTbgM)

Go here if you want the best cheesecake in town while playing a Nepali board game. Their game collection is small, but if you have a sweet tooth and a soft spot for games, Mellow is worth the trip.

### Khushi Coffee House

📍 [Google Maps](https://share.google/83DZP4WXkVjfc634D)

Khushi Coffee House might have the biggest board game collection in the city, from Ticket to Ride to Catan. The owner loves games and has made a cozy space where you can play for hours without any extra charge.

### ☕️ Daily Drip

📍 [Google Maps](https://share.google/cPyPdVcCpgk4Mvg5E)

A little café run by friendly owners who are happy to join you for a game. They have a good collection, and the coffee is excellent. If you go, try a few rounds of Guess Who with friends. Btw I've never lost a single round.

These spots work well for long, lazy game days when you don't want to host at home.
